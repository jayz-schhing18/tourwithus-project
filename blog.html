Longset Resort

  • Island: Koh Rong
  • TripAdvisor Rating: 4 Stars
  • Average Price / Night: $100

Koh Rong is famous for its serene beauty, often described as an 'island paradise' by visiting tourists. The island boasts beautiful pristine white beaches, clear turquoise ocean waters, and a tropical jungle wilderness containing several native species…

Longset Resort offers luxury accommodation on the beautiful Long Beach, to the South East side of Koh Rong. This beach is perfect for people who want to enjoy the spectacular views in a quiet relaxed environment. Our review team has visited this resort and rate it 4.5 / 5 – It is a Very Good place to stay if you want to lie back and admire the magnificent scenery in a more luxurious accommodation. In peak season, demand for all resorts is high, so please book your resort early if you are interested.

Tree House Bungalow Island

  • Island: Koh Rong
  • TripAdvisor Rating: 3 Stars
  • Average Price / Night: $40

Koh Rong is famous for its serene beauty, often described as an 'island paradise' by visiting tourists. The island boasts beautiful pristine white beaches, clear turquoise ocean waters, and a tropical jungle wilderness containing several native species…

Tree House Bungalow Resort is located just off the main tourist beach of Koh Rong (Koh Touch). Our review team has visited this resort and rate it 3.5 / 5 – It is a Good place to stay if you want to see the spectacular views in a more peaceful relaxing environment. In peak season, demand for all resorts is high, so please book your resort early if you are interested. Tree House Bungalows are situated at the far side of the bay – A 15 minute walk along the beach from the main pier (heading right as you get off the boat).
